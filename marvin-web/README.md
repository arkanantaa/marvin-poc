# Marvin Studio — 3D edition

Three.js geometry replaces the previous MP4 entrance. The model uses the original booth composition plus the supplied black transparent Marvin wordmark, olive velvet fabric and six photos extracted directly from `ape.pdf`. It faces directly forward, with a warm yellow backlit top sign, reflective champagne metal trim, a compact recessed stainless printer tray and an empty interior without a chair. The curtain has irregular soft folds and a gentle continuous breeze. Depth is inferred from the references.

Use the Node 24 release pinned in `../.nvmrc` (validated locally with 24.19.0).

```sh
npm ci
npm run dev
```

The site opens on a wall of drifting pictures (`src/components/MarvelScene.tsx`, using the React Bits `DriftWall` in `src/components/DriftWall.tsx`). It leans back 30° and stands up straight as you scroll, then fades into the booth. It lies over the booth and keeps its own scroll position (the page does not move), so everything about the booth below is unchanged; the booth takes over only on a new scroll gesture. Scrolling up in front of the booth brings the wall back. Pictures are listed in `src/data/marvel.ts`; pacing (scroll distance, glide, angle) is the block of constants at the top of `MarvelScene.tsx`. Direct links into the booth (`#work`, `#hello`) and `?intro=0` skip it.

Vite serves the website on port 5173. A single downward wheel gesture, upward touch swipe or PageDown starts a 3.6-second entrance: one continuous zoom while the curtain opens, with the portfolio page sitting behind the doorway and growing with the camera, so there is no white flash or fade; the booth's back wall is a depth-only “portal” through which the HTML page shows. An upward wheel gesture at the portfolio's top reverses home at the same slower pace. Scrolling within portfolio content remains native; “Our work” is an immediate shortcut. The entrance occupies one viewport of document scroll and can also be scrubbed with the scrollbar. A continuous forward/lateral path and logarithmic distance interpolation keep the approach from rushing near the curtain. Gentle wind continues at rest with the top rail pinned, capped at 30 fps and paused when hidden or beyond the entrance. The entrance ignores the OS reduced-motion setting. No video is requested. The renderer is dynamically imported only when needed, with a capped pixel ratio and resource cleanup. Missing WebGL and context loss retain a matching poster fallback.

Before entering, the booth is seen in a three-quarter view on a lit studio floor with a soft backdrop, a glowing sign and warm light spilling from inside (`src/scene/createBoothScene.ts`, `studioRig.ts`); the view drifts slightly with the pointer. Edit geometry and supplied texture crops in `src/scene/buildBooth.ts` (including the photo-strip display beside the curtain; the curtain length is `curtainDrop` in `src/scene/curtainMotion.ts`), cloth response and the hover “hand” in `src/scene/curtainMotion.ts` / `src/scene/createBoothScene.ts`, lighting/framing in `src/scene/createBoothScene.ts`, and choreography in `src/lib/timeline.ts`.

Inside the booth you first face the back wall, then the view turns left (a CSS 3D room, `.room` in `src/styles/site.css`) to the wall where the monitor hangs, with the console (selfie button and print slot) below it (`src/components/PortfolioMonitor.tsx`, `SelfiePrinter.tsx`); everything there is sized from one unit (`--u` in `src/styles/site.css`), so it looks the same on every device and only scales. The monitor shows one event at a time as Photos, a playable 3D booth or a Video. The photos ride round a ring you can drag or spin with the arrows; the small arrows and dots by the corner change event. One wheel notch or a short swipe walks you in. Once inside, the page is steady: the window is locked and the booth wall scrolls on its own frame, from the monitor down to the booking section only; the EXIT sign (or the logo) walks you back out. Events (IKEA, Wedding), their photos, videos, 3D booth colours, the IKEA event's own printed booth (`public/portfolio/booths/`) and the wedding backdrop (`public/portfolio/backdrops/`) live in `src/data/portfolio.ts`: each event's photos are `public/portfolio/events/<event>-01.webp`… (cropped 5:6), its clip and poster are in `public/portfolio/videos/`. The yellow selfie button flashes (with a synthesized shutter sound, `src/lib/sounds.ts`) and pushes a polaroid out of the black slot on the console, printed with the next of the real booth shots in `public/portfolio/selfies/` (`selfies` in `src/data/portfolio.ts`); it develops from dark to full colour and, three seconds after it is out, flips and spins away by itself. These selfie effects and the entrance play regardless of the OS reduced-motion setting. Scrolling down leaves the booth for the booking section: "Book for an event" opens WhatsApp, with Instagram and TikTok links. "Book a session" (in the header and in the booking section, or straight away from an address ending in `?book`) opens a full-screen form for a session at the walk-in booth (`src/components/BookSession.tsx`): strips, the "All your photos" add-on, the guest's name and whether they are at the booth now. The site has no payment server, so "Book and pay" opens WhatsApp with the order written out; the staff reply with the QRIS and the code. Prices and the address are in `booking` in `src/data/portfolio.ts` (set `extraStrip` once the price of extra strips is known). Fill in the WhatsApp number and the two profile links in `social` in `src/data/portfolio.ts`. Logos are `public/textures/marvin-wordmark.png` (monitor) and `public/textures/marvin-logo-studio.png` (header). Asset provenance and the Codex skill adaptation are recorded in `../docs/models/assets.md`.
