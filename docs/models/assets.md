The three JPEGs supplied on 2026-10-22 are copied byte-for-byte into `marvin-web/public/textures/`. The original attachments remain unchanged.

UV crop rectangles are explicit in `src/scene/buildBooth.ts`; no source image is overwritten. The printer housing has a real opening, sloping surround, sides and tray. The bottle/photo strip remain part of a recessed photographic insert, not separately reconstructed product meshes. The curtain's photo supplies color; independent fine grain supplies surface relief so photographed shadows are not mistaken for geometric depth.

`curtainMotion.ts` anchors the rail, varies fold shapes down the fabric and gives the free hem a bounded damped response to scroll input. This is procedural soft-cloth animation, not a collision-based cloth solver. The final gathered width remains clear of the camera, including during settling. The renderer stops once movement settles and does not run in the reduced-motion route.

`public/media/booth-realistic.jpg` is a 1920×980 browser capture of the revised model, generated by `tests/capture-model-details.mjs`. It replaces the old gold-curtain poster in loading/GPU/reduced-motion fallbacks.
